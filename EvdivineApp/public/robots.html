Current rules

The actual `robots.txt` file should stay minimal and direct.

  • User-agent: *

    Applies the policy to all crawlers.

  • Allow: /

    Lets public pages be crawled normally.

  • Sitemap: /sitemap.xml

    Points crawlers to the sitemap for discovery.
